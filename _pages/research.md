My research interests lie in computer vision and deep learning, with a particular focus on action quality assessment (AQA), human motion analysis and generation, and video understanding. I am drawn to the subtle language of human movement and the physical principles that shape it, with a special interest in physics-informed motion generation. My goal is to develop reliable and interpretable models that assess human performance, generate natural, physically plausible motion, and provide meaningful guidance to help people refine their movements and improve their skills.

For a full overview of my background, please see my [CV]({{ base_path }}/cv/). For research discussions, contact me at [ziqingch@buffalo.edu](mailto:ziqingch@buffalo.edu).
